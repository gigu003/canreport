# Foreword {-}

```{epoxy}
Cancer registration is a foundation of cancer prevention and control. Complete, accurate, and continuous registry data systematically describe cancer incidence, mortality, and survival in the registered population, characterize cancer patterns and temporal trends, and provide scientific evidence for etiological research, policy making, prevention and control planning, and evaluation of intervention effectiveness.

With the continued implementation of the Healthy China 2030 strategy, the role of cancer registration in cancer prevention and control has become increasingly important. Henan Province has actively promoted population-based cancer registration. By December 31, 2023, cancer registration had been implemented in all counties and districts across the province, forming a registry system that describes cancer incidence and mortality among urban and rural residents in Henan. Since 2013, Henan Cancer Center/Henan Cancer Hospital has released cancer registry statistics annually in the form of the Henan Cancer Registry Annual Report. The report has become an important source of evidence for cancer prevention and control in Henan Province and one of the landmark outputs of cancer registration in the province.

The {year.pub} Henan Cancer Registry Annual Report includes data for {year.data} submitted by {length(submit)} cancer registries, covering a population of {.num pop}, which accounted for {.percent pop/year.pop} of the total population of Henan Province in {year.data}.

The {year.pub} Henan Cancer Registry Annual Report consists of six chapters. Chapter 1 briefly introduces the development of the cancer registration system and the vital statistics system in Henan Province. Chapter 2 describes data sources and data collection methods. Chapter 3 presents indicators for registry data quality control, methods for quality evaluation, and the calculation of commonly used statistical indicators. Chapter 4 describes overall cancer incidence and mortality in Henan Province. Chapter 5 presents incidence and mortality by cancer site. Chapter 6 provides tabulated appendices containing the main incidence and mortality statistics for each cancer registry in Henan Province.

The successful publication of this report reflects the dedicated efforts of cancer registry staff across the province. We sincerely thank all institutions and individuals who contributed to and supported cancer registration in Henan Province.
```
